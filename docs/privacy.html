Prism 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

Prism 是一款在 iPhone 和 iPad 上播放本地视频和图片的 App。我们重视你的隐私：Prism 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

我们不收集的数据

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系。
  • 不包含广告、数据分析、崩溃统计或任何第三方追踪 SDK。
  • 不会把你的视频、图片、观看记录或设备信息发送到开发者或任何第三方服务器。
  • 不读取你的系统相册内容。

保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容只保存在 Prism 自己的存储空间里，卸载 App 时会一并删除：

  • 你导入的视频、图片和字幕文件，以及你建立的文件夹。
  • 缩略图缓存和媒体信息索引（时长、尺寸等）。
  • 偏好设置，例如排序方式、字幕大小、播完后的行为，以及视频的续播位置。

这些数据可能随你的 iCloud 或电脑备份一起被备份，这由你的系统设置决定，开发者无法访问。

App 请求的权限

  • 本地网络：仅在你打开“传文件”时使用。Prism 会在你的设备上开启一个只在同一 Wi-Fi 内可访问的接收服务，让电脑浏览器把文件直接传到手机。文件不经过任何外部服务器；关闭接收、离开页面或 App 进入后台时，服务即停止。
  • 添加到相册：仅在你主动选择“保存当前帧”到相册时使用，只写入，不读取相册。

儿童隐私

Prism 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

Prism Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Prism is a local video and image player for iPhone and iPad. Prism does not collect, upload or sell any personal data.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or account is required.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Your videos, images, viewing history and device information are never sent to the developer or any third-party server.
  • Prism does not read your Photos library.

Data stored on your device

The following stays inside Prism's own storage on your device and is removed when you delete the app: the videos, images, subtitles and folders you import; thumbnail caches and media metadata; and preferences such as sort order, subtitle size, end-of-playback behavior and resume positions. This data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups according to your system settings; the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

  • Local Network: used only while Wi-Fi Transfer is on. Prism runs a receiver on your device that is reachable only from your local Wi-Fi network, so a browser on your computer can send files directly to your phone. Files never pass through an external server, and the receiver stops when you turn it off, leave the screen or the app goes to the background.
  • Add to Photos: used only when you choose to save the current frame to Photos. Prism writes the image and never reads your library.

Children

Prism collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
